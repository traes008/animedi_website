# Terms (site vocabulary)

Use these names consistently. Full beginner guide: [`HOW_TO.md`](HOW_TO.md).

- **hero** — Large top-of-page visual. On home: slideshow area. On department pages: big header image.
- **department picture / department hero** — The department’s main photo (`assets/images/departments/<name>/hero/hero.jpg`). Shown at the top of that department’s pages **and** as the clickable block for that department on the home page.
- **slideshow** — Rotating images in the home hero (`assets/images/home/slideshow/`).
- **gallery** — Photo grid on a department’s “Foto’s” page (`…/gallery/`).
- **service image** — Photo next to one service/practice item (`…/services/`).
- **team photo** — Portrait next to a staff bio (`assets/images/team/`).
- **brand** — Logo and icons (`assets/brand/`).
- **alt text** — Short image description for accessibility/SEO.
